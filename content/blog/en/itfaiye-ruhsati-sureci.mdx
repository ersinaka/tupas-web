When you open a new business in Turkey, or renew an existing licence, one of the steps you may face is the **fire department compliance report** (*itfaiye uygunluk raporu*), often called the "fire permit". It confirms that your premises have the required fire safety measures in place.

This article outlines the process and how to prepare for the inspection.

## 1. Application

You apply to the fire department of the municipality where your premises are located. You will usually be asked for the address, intended use and floor area.

## 2. Preparing for the inspection

This is the stage that decides the outcome. Inspectors commonly look at:

- **Fire extinguishers:** the right type and number for the area and its risks, all within their service date.
- **Placement:** visible, reachable and mounted at a suitable height.
- **Signage:** emergency exit and fire equipment signs.
- **Emergency lighting:** escape routes stay lit during a power cut.
- **Escape routes:** exits open and unobstructed.
- **Systems:** where required, fire detection, sprinklers or kitchen hood suppression.

## 3. The inspection

The fire department visits your premises. If nothing is missing, the report is issued; if there are gaps, you are given time to fix them and a follow-up check is carried out.

## Common gaps

1. Extinguishers past their service date
2. No hood suppression system in a commercial kitchen
3. Missing or poorly placed exit signs
4. Emergency lights that do not work
5. Blocked hose cabinets or exit doors
