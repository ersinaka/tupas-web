An extinguisher can hang on the wall for years without being touched. Whether it works when you need it depends on regular servicing. Pressure loss, caking powder or a faulty valve can leave it useless at exactly the wrong moment.

This article covers servicing in three parts: the monthly check you can do yourself, periodic servicing by an authorised technician, and refilling.

## The monthly check: five minutes of your time

Do these checks once a month. If anything looks wrong, send the unit for service.

- **Gauge:** Is the needle in the green? A needle in the red means pressure has dropped or is too high.
- **Seal and pin:** Is the safety pin in place and the tamper seal intact? A broken seal may mean the unit has been used.
- **Body:** Any rust, dents or cracks?
- **Hose:** Any cracks, blockages or loose connections?
- **Location:** Is the unit in its place, visible and unobstructed?

## Annual servicing by an authorised technician

As a rule, extinguishers should be inspected by an authorised service **at least once a year**. The technician checks pressure, valve, hose and body in detail and replaces the agent if needed. A label showing the service date and the next due date is then attached to the unit.

## When does an extinguisher need refilling?

- **As soon as it has been used**, even briefly. A partly discharged unit may not be enough next time.
- When the gauge shows a loss of pressure.
- When servicing shows the agent has deteriorated.
- When the interval set for that type of extinguisher has passed.

Refill and hydrostatic test intervals depend on the type of extinguisher (powder, CO2, foam), its age and the applicable standard. We will share the right schedule for your units when we service them.

## Common mistakes

1. **Hiding the extinguisher behind a cupboard or door.** It must be visible and easy to reach.
2. **Putting a used extinguisher back on the wall.** Even a short discharge means it needs refilling.
3. **Ignoring the service label.** Units past their service date may be flagged at an inspection.
